Backend Engineer (Mid-level) — Payments Team

We're looking for a backend engineer to join our payments infrastructure team.
You'll design and operate services that process millions of transactions a
day, so experience with distributed systems, idempotency, and data
consistency under failure is essential. You should be comfortable with SQL
and at least one of Python, Go, or Java.

Responsibilities:
- Design APIs and services with strong consistency guarantees
- Debug production incidents and write postmortems
- Collaborate with product and compliance on payment flows

We value engineers who communicate tradeoffs clearly to non-technical
stakeholders and who mentor junior teammates.
