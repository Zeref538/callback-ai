Junior Full-Stack Engineer (New Grad)

Great first role for someone who has shipped at least one real project (class,
internship, or personal). You'll work across a Django backend and a React
frontend, with close mentorship from senior engineers. We care more about
fundamentals (data structures, debugging process, willingness to ask
questions) than years of experience.

You should be able to walk through a project you built end to end, including
decisions you'd make differently now.
