Privacy

Last updated 26 September 2026

callback-ai is a free practice tool for job interviews, built and run by John Andrei Martinez. This page says what happens to what you type into it. It describes what the code actually does; the code is public on GitHub if you want to check.

What you give it

  • A job post, and optionally a resume and a portfolio link.
  • Your answers to the interview questions, typed or spoken.
  • If you create an account: your email address, and your name if you sign in with Google.

There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

Who else sees it

  • NVIDIA. Your job post, resume, answers and the text of your portfolio page are sent to NVIDIA's AI service to write the questions and grade the answers. NVIDIA's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft. If the interviewer's voice is on, the text of each question (not your answers) is sent to Microsoft's text-to-speech service to be read aloud.
  • Google or Microsoft, for voice answers. Speaking an answer uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome that audio is processed by Google, and in Edge by Microsoft. Typing your answers avoids this.
  • Your portfolio site. If you give a portfolio link, our server visits that page once to read it.
  • Google Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google, which sees your IP address.
  • Firebase (Google), only if you create an account. Your sign-in details and saved interviews are stored in Firebase. Firebase's privacy information.
  • The hosts. The page is served by Netlify and the server runs on Render. Like any website, they see your IP address when you connect.

What is kept, and for how long

  • On our server: a running interview is held in memory, and its transcript is written to a file while it runs. Only the 200 most recent interviews are kept. When a newer one pushes an interview out, or the server restarts, it is gone. The server keeps no accounts.
  • Job posts: the list of skills worked out from a job post is saved on the server, so the same post isn't analysed twice. It holds the job post's contents, not yours.
  • As a guest: your past interviews are saved only in your own browser.
  • With an account: your past interviews are saved to your account until you delete them. Only you can read them.

Deleting your data

  • Guest: use Clear next to History in the sidebar.
  • Account: Clear deletes your saved interviews. Delete account, under your email in the sidebar, deletes your interviews and the account itself.
